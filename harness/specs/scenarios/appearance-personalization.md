title: Choose a comfortable reading style
type: scenario
requiredRules:
  - appearance-personalization
---

1. Open Settings → General and choose a theme and accent.
2. Adjust conversation size with the keyboard and choose a reading typeface. Inspect the sample.
3. Return to a conversation and read a question, a reply and inline code.
4. Reopen the same profile and check the saved choices.
5. Restore the appearance defaults and check that other preferences retain their values.
6. Navigate through every module in the Settings navigation registry. Compare page surfaces, then open a listbox and an actual provider dialog to inspect colors outside the scrolling page.
7. Compare standard and extra-large Settings text with the same conversation size. Validate keyboard selection, Escape, and narrow-window navigation.
8. At desktop width, compare the reading controls and live sample side by side on the initial viewport. At 375 CSS px and extra-large settings text, the sample follows the controls in a single column and all options fit.
